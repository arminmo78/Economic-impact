Historical parallels — which path are we on?
1929–1933 (Depression)
Debt deflation, austerity, bank failures, 25% unemployment. Avoided if: no credit system collapse, no competitive devaluations.
1970s (Stagflation)
Supply shocks + loose monetary policy. Required two recessions and Volcker's 20% rates to resolve. Current Iran oil shock echoes this.
1985–95 (Managed decline)
Plaza Accord USD devaluation, gradual fiscal consolidation. Avoided depression but 15+ years of adjustment. Dollar lost 40% vs major currencies.
1990s (Soft landing)
Clinton-era surplus, productivity boom, Greenspan soft landing. Dalio's only off-ramp. Requires bipartisan political will currently absent.
